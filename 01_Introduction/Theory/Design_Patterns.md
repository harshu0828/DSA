# Design Patterns

Design patterns are at an even higher level of abstraction than
abstract data types.

## Definition

Design patterns describe the design of algorithms rather than the
design of data structures.

They embody and generalize important design concepts that appear
repeatedly in many problem contexts.

## General Structure

Design patterns provide a general structure for algorithms while
leaving the details to be added as required for particular problems.

Design Pattern
      ↓
General algorithm structure
      ↓
Add problem-specific details
      ↓
Develop the required algorithm
